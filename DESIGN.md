## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, high-conviction aesthetic tailored for luxury real estate growth advisory and high-ticket capital deployment. It bridges the restraint of an architectural monograph with the rigor and clarity of performance marketing.

### Core Philosophy
- **Editorial Poise:** Typographic hierarchy takes center stage. Generous margins, asymmetric compositions, and intentional white space replace decorative visual clutter.
- **Architectural Discipline:** Layouts rely on structural hairline frames, continuous datum lines, and clear geometric rhythm, reminiscent of architectural blueprints and luxury print publications.
- **Conversion-Driven Restraint:** Performance elements (CTAs, metric dashboards, enquiry flows) remain razor-sharp and uncompromised, using high-contrast text and disciplined interaction models rather than flashy growth hacks.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Surfaces emulate archival paper, patinated bronze, and dark bespoke woodwork, evoking physical permanence and financial authority.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from luxury tactile materials—archival paper, ebonized wood, rich roasted leather, and patinated architectural hardware.

### Roles & Applications
- **Canvas (`#F4EFE7` - Warm Ivory):** The primary background color. Provides warm, non-glare editorial depth instead of stark digital white.
- **Primary Ink (`#151311` - Near-black):** High-contrast structural ink used for typography, key borders, high-impact buttons, and critical framing lines.
- **Prominent / Dark Surface (`#4A2E24` - Rich Espresso):** Applied to high-status inverted sections, dark luxury banners, key interactive focal points, and formal containers.
- **Architectural Accent (`#A47B55` - Muted Bronze):** Used sparingly for precision status markers, performance indicators, category pips, subtle rules, and active states.
- **Hairline / Border Ink (`rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.12)`): Creates quiet architectural division across grids without breaking compositional flow.
- **Inverted Canvas (`#151311` with `#F4EFE7` typography):** Reserved for prestige lead capture blocks, case study hero panels, and editorial testimonials.

## Typography

Typographic execution creates contrast between classical editorial authority and engineered performance precision.

### Pairing Rules
- **Display & Editorial Headlines:** Set in `Instrument Serif`. Retain regular weight to preserve delicate strokes, high ascenders, and classical elegance. Headlines should never appear crowded; allow baseline breathing room.
- **Body & Microcopy:** Handled exclusively by `DM Sans`. Clean geometry, wide open counters, and high x-height ensure flawless legibility across property dossiers, financial reporting, and complex navigation.
- **Metadata & Section Monograms:** Set uppercase metadata in `DM Sans` 600 weight with wide character spacing (`0.14em`) to mirror luxury catalog taxonomy.
- **Performance Data:** Key property valuation metrics and ROAS outputs utilize `DM Sans` with negative tracking, providing mathematical authority without defaulting to cold tech-oriented monospace fonts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

### Grid Structure & Scaling
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with `5rem` page margins and `2.5rem` gutters. Content maximum width is capped at 1440px to ensure editorial line-length comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 6-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. High-impact metrics collapse from 4-wide to 2x2 modular blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Full bleed is permitted for architectural photography, bounded by subtle hairline separators.

### Rhythm Principles
- Generous outer breathing room allows high-value developments and investment performance statistics to command full focus.
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) governs internal card anatomy, metadata clusters, and input elements.
- Macro-spacing (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) enforces generous breathing room between thematic sections, reinforcing an unhurried, luxury feel.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic blurs, and skeuomorphic gradients. Elevation is achieved through structural layering, contrast shifts, and refined physical boundaries.

### Architectural Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Foundation):** Warm Ivory canvas (`#F4EFE7`), raw and unadorned.
- **Tier 1 (Framed Containers & Cards):** Flat white or tonal canvas (`#FCFAF7`) outlined by an architectural hairline border (`1px solid rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.1)`). No shadow.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** Crisp white or deep espresso panels elevated by a bespoke ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.06)`. This mimics directional gallery lighting on fine paper.
- **Tier 3 (Inverted Dominance):** Full sections rendered in rich espresso (`#4A2E24`) or deep near-black (`#151311`), creating immediate visual grounding and dramatic vertical contrast along the scroll journey.

## Shapes

The design uses a sharp geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Geometry & Form
- **Zero Border Radius:** Every card, button, tag, and modal container terminates in crisp, 90-degree right angles. This mirrors bespoke architectural casework, marble stonework, and fine stationery.
- **Strict Linear Structure:** Visual cohesion relies entirely on clean perpendicular alignment and hairline frames (`1px`).
- **Pips & Micro Status Elements:** Circular geometry is permitted solely for functional micro-elements (such as 6px bronze live-status status pips and circular pagination counter dots) to create focused focal contrast against rectangular discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid near-black (`#151311`) background, warm ivory (`#F4EFE7`) text, 0px radius, 14px uppercase DM Sans with `0.1em` letter spacing. Padding: `16px 32px`. Hover shifts background to rich espresso (`#4A2E24`) with smooth color transition.
- **Secondary / Architectural Outline:** Transparent background, 1px solid near-black (`#151311`) border, near-black text. Hover inverts to solid near-black with ivory text.
- **Editorial Text Link:** DM Sans label followed by a fine directional arrow (`→`), underlined by a 1px border spaced 4px below baseline. Hover translates arrow 4px rightward without changing underline thickness.

### Cards
- **Property / Portfolio Card:** Tier 1 surface. High-ratio imagery flush to the top edge, enclosed in a 1px hairline border. The card body uses `space-md` internal padding, featuring uppercase metadata in bronze (`#A47B55`), headline in Instrument Serif, and bottom alignment for valuation figures.
- **Executive Metric Card:** Sharp container with a top hairline accent in bronze. Metric displayed in `data-metric` typography, anchored with a short uppercase label beneath.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Sharp rectangular containers with a subtle warm off-white surface (`#FAF7F2`) and a 1px perimeter border (`rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.18)`). Placeholder text in muted charcoal.
- **Active / Focus State:** Perimeter border transitions to near-black (`#151311`) with zero glow or fuzzy focus ring, maintaining crisp architectural delineation.
- **Field Labels:** Positioned above the field, set in `label-uppercase` style.

### Badges, Tags & Chips
- **Category & Status Tags:** Sharp rectangular enclosures. Background uses 6% tint of muted bronze or off-black; border is a matching 1px hairline; text is uppercase 10px DM Sans.
- **Live Deal Indicator:** Includes a 6px solid muted bronze circular pip paired with right-aligned uppercase status text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px by 18px square with a 1px solid near-black border and zero corner rounding. Checked state reveals a solid near-black fill containing a precise ivory check mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px by 18px sharp square with an inner 8px square fill when selected, avoiding rounded radio conventions to maintain cohesive angularity.

### Lists & Tables
- **Deal Flow & Transaction Rows:** Horizontal line-divided rows with `1px solid rgba(21, 19, 17, 0.1)`. Generous `24px` vertical padding. Hover states subtly shift row background to `#ECE5DB`.
- **Editorial Bullet Points:** Clean list items prepended by an em-dash (`—`) or hairline bronze horizontal mark rather than standard rounded bullets.

### Additional Signature Components
- **Property Spec Bar:** Fixed or inline horizontal strip detailing key metrics (square footage, expected yield, capital deployment) separated by vertical 1px rules.
- **Bespoke Consultation Drawer:** Slide-out lead generation panel on Tier 2 elevation, displaying a high-contrast espresso header block and focused single-column form fields.
